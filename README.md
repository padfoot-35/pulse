# Pulse: every supplier contract, decided before the deadline

A working AI prototype for contract renewals in the intelligent-repository stage of the contract lifecycle. Built as a concept for Sirion; not affiliated.

**Open it:** https://padfoot-35.github.io/pulse/

- **Story** explains why this stage, the stakeholders and their conflicts, the evidence, and the success metrics.
- **Product** has a guided 12-step tour across five roles, a narrated walkthrough video, and a mode to generate or upload a contract and watch Pulse read, score and add it to the workflow.

This copy runs without live AI (stored answers and a rules-based reader). The same prototype with live AI runs on Claude: https://claude.ai/artifact/QnBrTMmTd8EDzQWVsR9q3q

The company, contracts and user research are simulated from published evidence and labelled as such.
